1. Upload your Spotify data export
Your file is read on this device only. It is never uploaded. We keep a short summary (your top artists, top tracks, and rough listening totals) and only after you have seen it and said yes.
How do I get my export from Spotify?
  1. On a computer, sign in at spotify.com/account/privacy.
  2. Scroll to Download your data.
  3. Tick Account data (about the last year of listening) or Extended streaming history (your whole listening history), then click Request data.
  4. Confirm the request in the email Spotify sends you. Spotify tells you how long it will take. Extended history usually takes longer than Account data.
  5. When the second email arrives, download the .zip file and drop it below. You do not need to unzip it.
